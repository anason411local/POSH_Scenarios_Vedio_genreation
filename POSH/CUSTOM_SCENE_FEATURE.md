# Custom Scene Generation Feature

## 🎯 New Feature Added: Custom Single Scene Generation

The POSH Video Generation System now includes a powerful **Custom Scene Generation** mode that allows you to create targeted, specific training videos with complete control over the content.

## 🆚 Two Generation Modes

### 🤖 Automatic Full Scenario (Original)
- AI breaks down your scenario into 8 scenes automatically
- Generates all scenes in sequence (60-90 seconds total)
- Perfect for comprehensive training scenarios

### 🎯 Custom Single Scene (NEW!)
- Create one specific scene with your exact requirements
- Full control over dialogue, visuals, and system instructions
- Perfect for targeted training content and specific situations

## 🎬 Custom Scene Generation Process

### Step 1: Scene Configuration
- **Duration**: Configurable from 5-15 seconds (default: 8 seconds)

### Step 2: Visual Description (Required)
- **Visual Scene Description**: Detailed description of what should happen visually
- Include character actions, expressions, camera angles, workplace setting
- Describe inappropriate behavior clearly and realistically
- Specify authentic Indian corporate office environment

### Step 3: System Instructions (Optional)
- **Custom Instructions**: Specific directions for the AI video generator
- Camera angles, lighting preferences, cultural context
- Special effects, timing, or technical requirements
- Background sounds and audio specifications

## 💡 Tips for Better Custom Scenes

### Visual Descriptions
- Be very specific about character actions and expressions
- Include detailed visual descriptions of the workplace setting
- Describe the inappropriate behavior clearly and realistically
- Mention specific props or office elements (desk, computer, phone, etc.)
- Specify the emotional reactions of characters

### System Instructions
- Use for camera angles and visual focus ("Use close-up shots to show facial expressions")
- Specify lighting and atmosphere ("Bright office lighting", "Professional environment")
- Add cultural context ("Authentic Indian office environment with modern furniture")
- Include audio specifications ("Add realistic office sounds: keyboard typing, phone rings")
- Mention timing or pacing requirements

### Examples of Good Visual Descriptions
```
"In a modern Indian corporate office, a senior male manager approaches a junior female employee at her desk. He leans uncomfortably close and makes an inappropriate comment about her appearance. Show the employee's shocked and uncomfortable facial expression, her stepping back, and the manager's inappropriate smirking behavior. The office setting should be authentic with cubicles, computers, and other employees in the background."
```

### Examples of Good System Instructions
```
"Use close-up shots to show facial expressions during uncomfortable moments. 
Focus on authentic Indian office environment with modern furniture and typical corporate setup. 
Include natural background office sounds at low volume: keyboard typing, phone conversations, air conditioning."
```

```
"Medium shots to capture both characters' body language. Show the power dynamic through positioning - manager standing over seated employee. 
Add realistic office ambience and ensure lighting shows the discomfort clearly."
```

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Launch the Application**: Run `python run_streamlit.py` or `python -m streamlit run streamlit_app.py`
2. **Select Custom Mode**: Choose "🎯 Custom Single Scene" at the top
3. **Set Duration**: Choose video duration (5-15 seconds, default: 8)
4. **Describe the Scene**: Write detailed visual description of what should happen
5. **Add Instructions** (Optional): Include system instructions for camera, lighting, audio
6. **Generate**: Click "🎬 Generate Custom Scene" to create your video
7. **Review**: Watch the generated video and download if satisfied
